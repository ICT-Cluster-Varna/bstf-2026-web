3-Day Program

Main Track (Hall Bulgaria) + International Business Exchange (Hall Varna), running in parallel.

The program is subject to ongoing updates.

Day
Program Focus
Central Question
Expected Outcome
Day 15 October
New Opportunities for Growth and Efficiency Through AI and Technology
Why should businesses act now?
Leaders identify where technology can generate growth, efficiency, and competitive advantage - and which opportunities justify investment or require policy action.
Day 26 October
From Opportunity to Implementation: AI, Automation, and Secure Operations
What can be implemented, and how?
Operational and technical leaders explore practical applications, implementation approaches, financing instruments, and risk controls.
Day 37 October
New Horizons: Building the AI-Enabled Business of Tomorrow
What comes next?
Organizations understand the capabilities, infrastructure, and partnerships required for the next generation of AI-enabled business.

Policy Action: Based on the problem areas and recommendations identified during the Forum, a proposal for policy measures supporting AI adoption, digital infrastructure, workforce skills, innovation financing, cybersecurity, and cross-border cooperation in the Black Sea region will be developed and published.

Hall "Bulgaria" – Business Track

  • Day 1 — company owners, CEOs, board members, investors, senior strategy and innovation leaders, and production and technology executives responsible for investment priorities.
  • Day 2 — COOs, CTOs, CIOs, CISOs, production and operations managers, automation and data specialists, cybersecurity teams, export managers, and executives responsible for implementation.
  • Day 3 — company owners, CEOs, CIOs, CTOs, innovation leaders, digital transformation managers, process owners, project managers, and infrastructure specialists preparing the next stage of AI adoption.

Hall "Varna" – International Business Exchange Track

  • Day 1 — Belgium, France, and international cooperation in biotechnology, health care, and sustainable industries.
  • Day 2 — The Netherlands, Türkiye, and international cooperation in tourism and travel technologies.
  • Day 3 — The United Kingdom, with an emphasis on commercial partnerships, investment, technology transfer, and concrete joint initiatives.

Forum Policy Output

Based on the problem areas and recommendations identified during the three days, the Forum will develop and publish a proposal for policy measures supporting AI adoption, digital infrastructure, workforce skills, innovation financing, cybersecurity, and cross-border cooperation in the Black Sea region.
